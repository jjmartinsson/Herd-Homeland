Herd & Homeland

Animals, Ethics and Hard Choices in Wartime Sweden

A research game about the ethical choices that arise when war disrupts the care of animals and about how we weigh the welfare of animals against other important values.

About Research
About the Project

What is Herd & Homeland?

War confronts society with difficult ethical choices concerning animals. When feed, veterinary care and labour are no longer enough, farmers, veterinarians, authorities and animal owners are forced to make decisions without clear answers. How much risk can we ask people to take to help animals? How long should an animal be kept alive when its suffering cannot be relieved? These are hard ethical questions that have largely been left unanswered.

Herd & Homeland is a research project in which we make a serious computer game about these choices. The project brings together animal welfare science, ethics and game development and is carried out together with people who work with animal care, animal protection and crisis preparedness.

Our aim

To give the protection and welfare of animals a more natural place in war preparedness.

The project is funded by Formas and runs from November 2026 to October 2028.

Research

Research & Presentations

Herd & Homeland is both a game and a research instrument. When the game is played it shows how professionals and citizens prioritise when the welfare of animals is set against other values. The game and its anonymised data will be made openly available so that others can build on the project in research, policy and education.

Research Phases

Phase 1 Current
Nov 2026 – Mar 2027

Identify Dilemmas & Ethical Challenges

A review of research on animals in war and of regulations and preparedness plans. Based on this we are drafting dilemmas and scenarios that will be used in the game and in other kinds of comparative research.

Phase 2
Mar – Dec 2027

Interviews & Workshops

The dilemmas are tested and developed together with people who work with animal care, animal protection and crisis preparedness.

Phase 3
Apr 2027 – Mar 2028

Making the Game

The dilemmas become scenarios in a game that is developed and playtested.

Phase 4
Mar – Oct 2028

Game Release & Data Collection

The game is released to the public. The data is analysed and shared openly.

More information about the game, presentations, publications and research data will be published here as the project progresses.

Do you want to contribute with your perspective?

The dilemmas in the game are identified and tested together with the people who would face them. We are therefore interested in the perspectives of everyone who works with animals, animal protection or crisis preparedness. This includes but is not limited to farmers, veterinarians, staff at zoos and animal shelters, animal owners and animal welfare organisations, as well as people who work with crisis preparedness and civil defence at the local, regional and national level.

If you want to take part you can send an e-mail and tell us briefly who you are and how you work with these questions. We may then invite you to an interview or a workshop where we discuss the dilemmas together. You can choose to be anonymous and participation is always voluntary.
