Contribute

Share Your Perspective

The dilemmas in Herd & Homeland are not imposed from the outside. They are identified and tested together with the people who would face them. If you work with animals, animal welfare or preparedness, your experience can help make the research both sound and meaningful.

Who we want to hear from

Everyone with a stake in animal care during war

Farmers & animal keepers

Those who would have to keep animals fed, watered and cared for when supply chains fail.

Veterinarians

Including animal health staff who would face scarce medicines and impossible priorities.

Zoo & shelter staff

People responsible for animals that cannot easily be moved, released or rehomed.

Pet & horse owners

Citizens whose companion and hobby animals depend on them in a crisis.

Military & service-animal handlers

Handlers of dogs, horses and other animals in the armed forces, police and rescue services.

Authorities & civil defence

Those who write and follow preparedness plans at national, regional and municipal level.

Animal welfare organisations

Organisations and volunteers working for the protection and welfare of animals.

Reindeer herders & others

Anyone else with experience of semi-domesticated animals or a perspective we may have missed.

How it works

Ways to contribute

  1. Get in touchSend an e-mail telling us briefly who you are and how you work with animals or preparedness. You don't need to have thought it all through.
  2. Interviews & workshopsWe may invite you to an interview or a focus group workshop, where we discuss and test dilemmas and scenarios. Participation is voluntary and you can withdraw at any time.
  3. PlaytestingLater in the project, you can help us test the game before it is released to the public.
