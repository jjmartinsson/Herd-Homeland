Contribute

Contribute with your perspective

The dilemmas in Herd & Homeland are identified and tested together with the people who would face them. Your experience can help make the game and the research both realistic and meaningful.

Who can contribute?

We would like to hear from you

We are interested in the perspectives of everyone who works with animals, animal protection or crisis preparedness. This includes farmers, veterinarians, staff at zoos and animal shelters, animal owners, animal welfare organisations, authorities and civil defence.

If you want to contribute you can send an e-mail and tell us briefly who you are and how you work with these questions. We may then invite you to an interview or a workshop. Participation is voluntary and you can withdraw at any time.
